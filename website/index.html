How It Works

From Chest to Screen.
In Under a Second.

💓

Skin → Chip

Two electrodes capture a ~1 mV heart signal. The AD8232 amplifies it 100×, filters noise, outputs a clean 0–3.3V wave.

⚡

Sampled 250× per Second

A hardware timer fires every 4 ms — a metronome that cannot be distracted. Each tick reads the analog voltage as a 12-bit number.

📦

Packed into 20-Byte Envelopes

Every 8 samples are sealed into a BLE-friendly packet. 31.25 envelopes per second — a steady stream of postcards.

📡

Bluetooth LE Flight

ESP32's radio pushes each envelope wirelessly. No pairing dance, no cables. The phone subscribes once and data flows.

🔍

Phone Catches & Checks

The app validates every frame: wrong length → discard. Sequence gap → count it. Out-of-range vitals → reject.

🎯

Triage & Explanation

Rule engine evaluates thresholds → risk band → plain-language explanation. Offline-first, labelled honestly, actionable.

The Hardware

SSAI-SENSE-01.
Hospital Screening in Your Pocket.

A custom sensor board with cross-shaped 3D-printed enclosure. Under ₹6,000 total cost.

Drag to rotate • Scroll to zoom
📡

ESP32-WROOM-32

Dual-core MCU, 250 Hz sampling, R-peak detection, BLE radio, FreeRTOS dual-core task split

📊

AD8232

Single-lead ECG front end — amplifies 1 mV heart signal with lead-off detection

💓

MAX30102

Pulse oximetry — HR + SpO₂ via reflectance PPG on separate I²C bus

MLX90614

Contactless IR temperature — ±0.2°C with +2°C core compensation

MPU6050

6-axis IMU — fall detection surviving screen-off

🔋

TPS63020 Buck-Boost

Flat 3.3V across entire Li-ion discharge. 19.8h streaming / 11mo fall-watch
