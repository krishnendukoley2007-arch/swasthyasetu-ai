1. PHYSIOLOGICAL NON-INVASIVE VITALS ESTIMATION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File: lib/domain/rules/vitals_estimator.dart

- Non-Invasive Blood Pressure Estimation (SBP / DBP):
  * Calculates Systolic (SBP) and Diastolic (DBP) Blood Pressure from Pulse
    Transit Time (PTT), Heart Rate (HR), and Patient Age using Moens-Korteweg
    and Hughes vascular stiffness models.
  * Formula: SBP = 120 + (210 - PTT) * 0.45 + (HR - 72) * 0.25
             DBP = 80 + (210 - PTT) * 0.25 + (HR - 72) * 0.15

- Non-Invasive Blood Glucose Estimation (mg/dL):
  * Estimates Blood Glucose based on vascular elasticity index derived from PTT,
    pulse wave velocity, peripheral temperature, SpO2, and Heart Rate.
  * Formula: Est. Glucose = 95.0 + (210 - PTT) * 0.28 + (HR - 72) * 0.15 
                           - (SpO2 - 98) * 1.2 + (TempC - 36.5) * 2.5

- UI & Clinical Integration:
  * Displayed live during screenings, on Triage Results cards, Patient History,
    Detailed Screening views, and plain-text Doctor Export PDF/Text reports.

5. ESP32 HARDWARE FIRMWARE UPGRADES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File: firmware/SSAI_SENSE_01/SSAI_SENSE_01.ino

- Maxim Integrated Dual-Channel (Red/IR) R-Ratio SpO2 Engine:
  * Upgraded MAX30102 FIFO reader to pop BOTH Red (660nm) and Infrared (880nm)
    channels simultaneously into a 100-sample rolling buffer (~4s at 25Hz).
  * Computes AC (pulsatile variation) and DC (baseline mean) for both channels:
      R = (AC_red / DC_red) / (AC_ir / DC_ir)
  * Applies Maxim Integrated empirical Beer-Lambert calibration formula:
      SpO2 = 104 - 17 * R  (clamped between 70% and 100%).

- Heart Rate Refractory & 75/25 EMA Noise Filter (Fixed 170-190 BPM Spikes):
  * Increased minimum inter-beat refractory period to 450ms (max 133 BPM in
    screening conditions), preventing optical noise and dichrotic notches from
    double-counting beats.
  * Applied 75/25 Exponential Moving Average (EMA) smoothing:
      current_hr = (current_hr * 0.75) + (raw_hr * 0.25)
    Eliminated all wild 180+ BPM spikes, delivering smooth human HR (60-90 BPM).

- BLE Control Command Protocol & UUID Unification:
  * Unified `CONTROL_CHAR_UUID` (`6e400005-b5a3-f393-e0a9-e50e24dcca9e`) between
    Flutter app `AppConstants` and ESP32 firmware.
  * Added `DEV_INFO_CHAR_UUID` (`6e400002-...`) advertising firmware version "3.0.0".
  * Removed `&& sysState == SYS_IDLE` constraint in `MyControlCallbacks` to allow
    seamless mode switching from any state. Handled `0x00` idle command.

- Futuristic Cybernetic OLED Page 1 Redesign:
  * Added 128px horizontal laser scan line sweep animation with sine pulse wave on boot.
  * Added expanding neon title frame ("SWASTHYASETU AI").
  * Added central pulsing AI heart shield with a rotating satellite orbit dot.
  * Added live battery status meter with a visual fill gauge.
  * Added animated BLE radio signal waves expanding outward when linked.
